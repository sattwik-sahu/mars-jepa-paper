\subsection{Backbone and Decoder Compatibility}
\label{sec:backbone-dec-compat}

A comparison of segmentation performance of different segmentation decoders using the I-JEPA and our SS-JEPA encoder backbones is presented in \cref{tab:ssl-enc-seg}. SS-JEPA with the UPerNet~\cite{DBLP:journals/corr/abs-1807-10221} decoder demonstrates the best performance of $0.865$ mIoU. Meanwhile I-JEPA performs the best with the SegFormer~\cite{DBLP:journals/corr/abs-2105-15203} decoder, achieving $0.837$ mIoU.
