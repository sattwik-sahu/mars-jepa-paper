\subsection{Model Complexity and Efficiency}
\label{sec:model-compl-eff}

The relationship between segmentation accuracy and computational complexity is illustrated in \cref{fig:perf-size-comp}, where the SS-JEPA + UPerNet configuration defines the Pareto frontier. While achieving a competitive $0.865$ mIoU, the model utilizes only 20M parameters, representing a $5.6\times$ reduction in size compared to the 113M parameter DualSwinV2 ensemble~\cite{kabir2026dualswinfusionsegmultimodalmartianlandslide}. The marginal performance gain of $0.002$ mIoU provided by the ensemble does not justify the disproportionate increase in computational overhead, establishing the proposed framework as a highly efficient alternative for planetary remote sensing.

The parameter efficiency of the SS-JEPA backbone is further evidenced by its performance relative to supervised baselines and the I-JEPA variant. Specifically, our model outperforms the TinySwin + FPN baseline (33M parameters) by $2.8\%$ mIoU despite being significantly smaller. Furthermore, the transition from I-JEPA to SS-JEPA results in a $15.6\%$ parameter reduction alongside a $2.8\%$ mIoU gain. These results indicate that the spatio-spectral masking strategy enables the encoder to internalize cross-modal dependencies more effectively than visible-spectrum supervised objectives or spatial-only SSL.
