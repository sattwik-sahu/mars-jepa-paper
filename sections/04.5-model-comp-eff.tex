\subsection{Model Complexity and Efficiency}
\label{sec:model-compl-eff}

The relationship between segmentation accuracy and computational complexity is illustrated in \cref{fig:main_results}, where the SS-JEPA + UPerNet configuration defines the Pareto frontier. Despite being 5.6$\times$ smaller than the 113M parameter DualSwinV2 ensemble~\cite{kabir2026dualswinfusionsegmultimodalmartianlandslide}, our 20M parameter model achieves a competitive 0.865 mIoU. Furthermore, as shown in \cref{fig:perf-gflops-comp}, this efficiency extends to inference cost, with our model requiring 120.5 GFLOPs compared to the ensemble's 135 GFLOPs. The ensemble's marginal lead of 0.2\% mIoU requires a massive increase in model size alongside a higher computational footprint, making our single-model approach a more practical candidate for the low-power hardware typical of planetary exploration platforms.

The parameter efficiency of the SS-JEPA backbone is further evidenced by its performance relative to supervised baselines and the I-JEPA variant. Specifically, our model outperforms the TinySwin + FPN baseline (33M parameters) by $2.8\%$ mIoU despite being significantly smaller. Furthermore, the transition from I-JEPA to SS-JEPA results in a $15.6\%$ parameter reduction alongside a $2.8\%$ mIoU gain. These results indicate that the spatio-spectral masking strategy enables the encoder to internalize cross-modal dependencies more effectively than visible-spectrum supervised objectives or spatial-only SSL.
