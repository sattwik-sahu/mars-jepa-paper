\subsection{Qualitative Evaluation and Interpretability}
\label{sec:qual-eval}

This section provides a qualitative analysis of the segmentation performance of models using the JEPA encoder backbones, and the latent representations learned by the JEPA encoders through comprehensive visualizations.

\paragraph{Segementation Performance}
A qualitative comparison of segmentation masks predicted by the best-performing models from \cref{sec:backbone-dec-compat} is presented in \cref{fig:seg-comp}. The first four columns present the different multispectral input bands (RGB, digital elevation model (DEM), slope and thermal inertial map), and the last three columns compare the segmentation masks from I-JEPA + SegFormer, SS-JEPA + UPerNet and the ground truth from the dataset respectively, overlayed on the grayscale band. The SS-JEPA + UPerNet model consistently shows lower false positives.
