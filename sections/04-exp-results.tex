\section{Experimental Evaluation and Results}
\label{sec:exp-res}

In this section, we provide a comprehensive empirical evaluation of the Mars-JEPA framework landslide segmentation from multispectral images. We experimentally evaluate the quality of the semantic representations from encoders and validate the superiority of representations from our SS-JEPA, adapted to the multispectral image domain. The experimental setup is described in \cref{sec:exp-setup}, including the dataset and evaluation protocols, while the implementation details for pre-training and downstream segmentation models is provided in \cref{sec:impl-det}. An extensive ablation study is then presented in \cref{sec:backbone-dec-compat} to identify the optimal pairing of JEPA encoder backbones and segmentation decoders. Subsequently, the best-performing models are compared against established fully-supervised baselines and current state-of-the-art in Martian landslide segmentation, in \cref{sec:comp-sota}. Finally, we analyze the computational efficiency of our approach in comparison to the SOTA in \cref{sec:model-compl-eff} and provide a qualitative analysis of the latent representations learned by our JEPA encoders and the segmentation masks from the best-performing segmentation models in \cref{sec:qual-eval}.
