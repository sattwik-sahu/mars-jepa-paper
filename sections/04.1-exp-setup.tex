\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:exp-setup}

The evaluation of our framework proposed in \cref{sec:methodology} is done on the MMLSv2 dataset~\cite{mmlsv2_2026}. The dataset contains co-registered 128*128 GeoTIFF tiles featuring seven heterogeneous channels: Thermal Inertia (THEMIS, ~100m/pixel), Slope (derived from DEM), Digital Elevation Model (MOLA/HRSC, ~200m/pixel), Grayscale Imagery (CTX, ~6m/pixel), and a three-channel RGB basemap (Viking mosiac, ~232m/pixel). The publicly available split of the dataset contains 465 labeled training samples and 66 labeled validation samples.
