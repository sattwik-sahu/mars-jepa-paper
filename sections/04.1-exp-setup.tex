\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:exp-setup}
The proposed framework described in \cref{sec:methodology} was evaluated on the MMLSv2 dataset~\cite{mmlsv2_2026}, a multimodal Martian landslide segmentation benchmark consisting of co-registered \(128 \times 128\) GeoTIFF tiles. Each sample contains seven heterogeneous sensing channels, including Thermal Inertia (THEMIS, \( \sim \mppx{100} \), Slope maps derived from DEM data, Digital Elevation Models (MOLA/HRSC, \( \sim \mppx{200} \), Grayscale imagery from CTX (\( \sim \mppx{6} \)), and a three-channel RGB basemap generated from Viking mosaics (\( \sim \mppx{232} \)).

The publicly available split of the dataset contains \(465\) labeled training samples and \(66\) labeled validation samples. Owing to the heterogeneous nature of the sensing modalities and the varying numerical distributions across channels, all multispectral inputs were preprocessed using channel-wise normalization prior to training. All training and inference was performed on a single NVIDIA RTX A4500 GPU with \( \qty{20}{\giga\byte} \) VRAM.
